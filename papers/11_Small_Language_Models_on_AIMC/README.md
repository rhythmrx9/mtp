# 11 · Small Language Models on Analog / NVM In-Memory Hardware

Running and evaluating (small) language models (BERT-family, GPT-2, Llama-1B–8B, Phi, ternary/1-bit LLMs, KV caches) on analog or non-volatile in-memory hardware (PCM, ReRAM, FeFET/FeRAM, MRAM, gain cells), on silicon or under its non-idealities: hardware-aware training, adapters, noise robustness, mapping and accelerator design.
