# 01 · Surveys & Foundations

Reviews and perspectives that define in-memory computing with resistive / phase-change devices, the memory-wall motivation, device requirements, and the state of analog AI hardware.

16 papers, 14 with a local PDF. Open `../index.html` for summaries, claims, remarks and citation contexts.
