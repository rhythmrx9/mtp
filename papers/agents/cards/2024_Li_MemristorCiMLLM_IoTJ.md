## TL;DR
Analyses memristor mobility and drive-frequency effects on state transitions and discusses implications for analog vs digital CiM in LLMs for IIoT.

## Summary
The paper explores connections between memristor-based CiM and LLMs for industrial IoT. It reports that memristor mobility and applied-voltage frequency are key parameters of state transitions and examines instant saturation and transition dynamics. It compares multistate (analog CiM) and dual-state (digital CiM) memristors in terms of accuracy and power. Based on abstract only.
