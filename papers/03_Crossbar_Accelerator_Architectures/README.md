# 03 · Crossbar Accelerator Architectures

Architecture-level designs that build DNN accelerators out of crossbar tiles (ISAAC, PRIME, PipeLayer, PUMA and successors): tiles, pipelines, ISAs, heterogeneous analog+digital systems.

15 papers, 10 with a local PDF. Open `../index.html` for summaries, claims, remarks and citation contexts.

| 2021 | **BRAHMS** BRAHMS: Beyond Conventional RRAM-based Neural Network Accelerators Using Hybrid Analog Memory System | DAC | BRAHMS eliminates redundant analog-to-digital conversions in RRAM-based CNN accelerators by fusing post-MAC operations (shift-add, pooling, activation, etc.) into analog resistive content-addressable memory (ARCAM) arrays, reporting several-fold throughput and >10x energy-efficiency gains over an ISAAC-like baseline. | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/dac18074.2021.9586247) (no local PDF) |

| 2020 | **TIMELY** Timely: Pushing Data Movements And Interfaces In Pim Accelerators Towards Local And In Time Domain | ISCA | TIMELY cuts input/Psum data-movement and DAC/ADC energy in ReRAM PIM with analog local buffers, time-domain interfaces and only-once-input-read mapping, achieving 21 TOPs/W (8-bit) peak, over 10x PRIME energy efficiency and up to 736.6x throughput in simulation with <=0.1% accuracy loss. | [2020_Li_TIMELY_ISCA.pdf](2020_Li_TIMELY_ISCA.pdf) |
| 2019 | **CASCADE** CASCADE | MICRO | CASCADE cascades MAC RRAM arrays into buffer RRAM arrays through TIAs so partial sums are buffered and accumulated in analog (R-Mapping), cutting A/D conversions from 256 to 10 per 16 cycles and giving 3.5x lower energy than an ADC-based and 11.0x lower than an SA-based in-RRAM design at competitive throughput. | [2019_Chou_CASCADE_MICRO.pdf](2019_Chou_CASCADE_MICRO.pdf) |

| 2019 | **FloatPIM** FloatPIM | DAC | FloatPIM is a fully digital ReRAM PIM architecture that performs floating-point (bfloat16/fp32) CNN training and inference with in-memory NOR operations, achieving on average 303.2x speedup and 48.6x energy gain over a GTX 1080 for training and 4.3x/15.8x over PipeLayer. | [2019_Imani_FloatPIM_DAC.pdf](2019_Imani_FloatPIM_DAC.pdf) |
