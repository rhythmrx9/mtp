# 04 · Transformer & Attention Accelerators (CIM / PIM)

Architectures that accelerate transformers and self-attention on crossbars and CIM/PIM: dynamic-operand attention, write-endurance, hybrid analog/digital and 3D designs. Papers whose focus is deploying language models themselves are in folder 11.

27 papers, 16 with a local PDF. Open `../index.html` for summaries, claims, remarks and citation contexts.
